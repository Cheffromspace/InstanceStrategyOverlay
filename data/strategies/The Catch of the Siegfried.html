Siegfried
Act 1
- Bring Aetheric Mimicry, Exuviation, and physical, fire, wind, and ice spells.
- Magic Drain: 🌟 Magitek Field buff reflects magic damage. Use physical spell (e.g., Drill Cannons).
- Ankle Graze: 🤢 Inflicts Bind. Cleanse with Exuviation.
- Hyperdrive: 💥 Player-centered AoE, cast multiple times. 🏃 Avoid.
- Magitek Explosive: 💥💥 8 bomb AoEs in square pattern. 🏃 to safe corner.
- Rubber Bullet: 💨 Knockback. Use to reach safe spot from Magitek Explosive.
Act 2
- Law of the Torch: 💥📐 3 large cone AoEs. 🏃 between cones.
- Swiftsteel: 💨💥 Knockback + scattered AoEs in donut pattern. Surecast to avoid knockback, dodge AoEs.
- Sparksteel: 💥🔥 Point-blank AoE + fire puddles. 🏃 Avoid both.
- Shattersteel: 🧊 Ice crystals inflict Deep Freeze stacks. Stay away from crystals.
Act 3
- Clones appear, vulnerable to ice, fire or wind spells. Focus on real Siegfried unless going for achievement.
- All previous mechanics continue. Clones use same attacks as Siegfried.
