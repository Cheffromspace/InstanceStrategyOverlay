Gogo
Act 1
- Do not attack Gogo during 🌟 Mimic buff.
- Mimicked Flamethrower: 💥📐 Conal fire attack. Heal through.
- Mimicked Sap: 💥🎯 3 successive AoEs. 🏃 Keep moving, use Sprint.
- Mimicked Imp Song: 🤢 Transforms player. Interrupt with Bad Breath/Flying Sardine.
- Mimicked Doom Impending: ☠️ Incurable + Doom. Heal to full before cast ends.
- Mimicked Bunshin: 👾 Imitation Gogo. 🏃 Circle imitation to dodge 💥📏💥📐 attacks.
- Mimicked Raw Instinct: 🌟 Critical Strikes buff. Eerie Soundwave immediately after cast.

Act 2
- Gogo Fire III: 💥💥 Arena AoE + Pyretic. Don't move/act during debuff.
- Gogo Blizzard III: 💥🎯 Circle AoE on Gogo. Stay away from boss.
- Gogo Thunder III: ⚡ Persistent electric AoE. Avoid affected area.
- Gogo Flare/Holy: 💥💥 Consecutive arena AoEs. Heal between or 🛡️ Diamondback.
- Gogo Meteor: ☄️ Complex AoE/proximity mechanic. 🏃 Dodge, 🛡️ Diamondback lethal meteor.
- Charybdis: 🌪️ Pulsing tornado AoEs. Stand between tornadoes when safe.
- Icestorm: 🧊 Heavy + Frostbite debuffs. Exuviation to remove, prioritize Heavy.
- Gogo Thunder III: ⚡⚡⚡ 3 consecutive casts. Bait into tornado gaps, 🏃 Sprint to avoid.
