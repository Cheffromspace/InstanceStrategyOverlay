Azulmagia
Act 1
- Only use magic attacks after the first move. Watch for reflected autoattacks.
- Ice Spikes: 🌟 Permanent physical-reflection barrier.
- The Ram's Voice: 💥 Untelegraphed circle AoE, inflicts Deep Freeze.
- The Dragon's Voice: 💥🍩 Untelegraphed donut AoE, inflicts Paralyze. Always follows The Ram's Voice.
- Plaincracker: 💥💥 Circle AoE + donut AoEs, 🤮⬆️ Vulnerability Up. 🏃 Move quickly, boss may use other abilities during donut AoEs.
- Apocalyptic Bolt: 💥📏 Line AoE, 🤮⬆️ Vulnerability Up.
- Apocalyptic Roar: 💥📐 Cone AoE, 🤮⬆️ Vulnerability Up.

Act 2
- Repelling Spray: 🌟 Permanent magic-reflection barrier. Beware reflected damage from non-damaging spells.
- Blazing Angon: 👾 Summons adds that inflict Burns (stacking DoT). Destroy with physical attacks.

Act 3
- Boss switches between barriers. Electric puddle at arena edge.
- Charybdis: 💥💥 Four tornadoes cast circle AoEs, reduce visibility.
- Web: 🤢 Severe Heavy debuff. 🏃 Move to edge, use Loom.
- Meteor: ☠️ Massive damage, likely one-shot. Use Loom to reach edge or tank with Diamondback. Always follows Web. Leaves flame puddle.
